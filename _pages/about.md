I’m Yuxuan Luo (罗宇轩), a Ph.D. student at the Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology, Peking University, advised by [Prof. Zhouhui Lian](https://www.icst.pku.edu.cn/zlian/). I studied Artificial Intelligence at [Yuanpei College](https://yuanpei.pku.edu.cn/en/aboutyuanpei/collegeprofile/index.htm), Peking University.

My research focuses on **how images can encode knowledge precisely and communicate it clearly**. I study how to interpret and generate these images, and how to evaluate whether readers can recover the intended meaning.

I’m currently a research intern in the Multimedia Computing Group at Microsoft Research Asia, where I pursue this work. I also have hands-on experience developing multimodal foundation models.
