Yuxuan Luo

Ph.D. student · Peking University

I’m Yuxuan Luo (罗宇轩), a Ph.D. student at the Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology, Peking University, advised by Prof. Zhouhui Lian. I studied Artificial Intelligence at Yuanpei College, Peking University.

I study how models read and generate images that carry knowledge. My work ranges from recovering brush movements in Chinese calligraphy to generating scientific diagrams whose arrows and labels preserve the relationships in a paper. I also build benchmarks to test whether those visual details survive generation.

I use fine-tuning, instruction tuning, and evaluation to study how multimodal models reason about visual structure, including what generative models learn about the world. At Microsoft Research Asia, I co-authored the Mage-Flow and Mage-VL technical reports on image generation, editing, and streaming video understanding.

I welcome collaborations on knowledge-intensive visual media. Email me · Google Scholar · GitHub

Research collaborations

  1. Mage-Flow

    A native-resolution diffusion model for generating images from text and editing them with instructions. Its four-step Turbo variant generates a 1024 × 1024 image in 0.59 seconds on an A100.

    Paper Project Code Gallery

  2. Mage-VL

    A streaming vision-language model that uses video motion vectors and residuals to encode changing regions, reducing the visual tokens needed to follow a scene over time.

News

Jul 20, 2026 SciForma: Structure-Faithful Generation of Scientific Diagrams was accepted by SIGGRAPH Asia 2026! SciForma is a framework that generates scientific methodology diagrams with structural faithfulness across components, arrows, and text.
Sep 19, 2025 MMMG was accepted by Neurips 2025! MMMG is a large-scale discipline-image benchmark designed to assess text-to-image (T2I) models on their ability to generate faithful and readable visuals. Our cases span 10 disciplines and 6 educational levels.
Jun 25, 2025 CalliReader: Contextualizing Chinese Calligraphy via an Embedding-aligned Vision Language Model was accepted by ICCV 2025! This paper proposes CalliReader, a novel VLM that solves Chinese Calligraphy Contextualization. We also release the first page-level Calligraphy dataset and CalliBench.
Apr 06, 2024 CalliRewrite: Recovering Handwriting Behaviors from Calligraphy Images without Supervision has been selected as a finalist for the IEEE ICRA 2024 Best Paper Award in Service Robotics! This research was conducted during my undergraduate studies under the guidance of Prof. Zhouhui Lian.
