**CalliReader: Contextualizing Chinese Calligraphy via an Embedding-aligned Vision Language Model** was accepted by **ICCV 2025**! This paperproposes **CalliReader**, a novel plug-and-play VLM that solves Chinese Calligraphy Contextualization problem. We also release the first page-level Chinese Calligraphy dataset and CalliBench.
