**MMMG: A Massive, Multidisciplinary, Multi-Tier Generation Benchmark for Text-to-Image Reasoning** was accepted by **Neurips 2025**! MMMG is a large-scale discipline-image benchmark designed to assess text-to-image (T2I) models on their ability to generate faithful and visually readable visuals based on knowledge-intensive while concise prompts. We collect 4,456 test cases, spanning multiple academic disciplines and educational levels. We also propose FLUX-Reason as an intermediate approach on discipline image generation.
