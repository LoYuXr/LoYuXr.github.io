**CalliReader: Contextualizing Chinese Calligraphy via an Embedding-aligned Vision Language Model** was accepted by **ICCV 2025**! This paper proposes **CalliReader**, a novel VLM that solves Chinese Calligraphy Contextualization. We also release the first page-level Calligraphy dataset and CalliBench.
